Ananth K S

I am a final-year Computer Science undergraduate at the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IITH), advised by Prof. Vineeth N Balasubramanian at the Machine Learning and Vision Lab. My research interests lie in continual learning, multimodal learning, and mechanistic interpretability. More broadly, I am interested in learning systems that can continuously acquire knowledge while remaining robust, data-efficient, and interpretable.

Previously, I was a Data and Applied Scientist Intern at Microsoft AI and a research intern in the Department of Computer Science and Automation at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), where I worked with Prof. Shirish K Shevade.

Research

Annotation-Free Class-Incremental Learning
Hari Chandana Kuchibhotla*, Ananth K S*, Vineeth N Balasubramanian
ArXiv 2025 (* equal contribution)
description

We study annotation-free class-incremental learning, where models must learn from sequentially arriving unlabeled data. Our framework aligns visual and semantic structure using external world knowledge and uses privacy-preserving replay to reduce catastrophic forgetting without storing private user data.
